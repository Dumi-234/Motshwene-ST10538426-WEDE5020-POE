About Cover Drive

Our Story

Cover Drive was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate cricketers who were frustrated by the lack of accessible, high-quality cricket equipment in their local community. Starting as a small online store operating out of a garage, the business has expanded to a dedicated warehouse and now supplies teams and players across the province.

Our Mission

To empower cricketers of all levels by providing access to top-quality, affordable cricket equipment and expert advice, fostering a love for the game from the grassroots up.

Our Vision

To become the leading online destination for cricket equipment in South Africa, known for our extensive product range, unbeatable customer service, and commitment to growing the sport.

  • Community: We support local cricket at every level.
